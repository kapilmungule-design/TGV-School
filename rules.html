📚 School Rules

Discipline | Respect | Responsibility

🎓 Student Discipline

  • Students should maintain discipline inside and outside the school campus.
  • Students must respect teachers, staff and classmates.
  • Students should follow school timings regularly.
  • Students must carry proper school uniform and identity card.

📖 Academic Rules

  • Complete homework and assignments on time.
  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Maintain proper notebooks and study materials.
  • Participate actively in academic activities.

🏫 Classroom Rules

  • Keep classroom clean and organized.
  • Listen carefully during teaching sessions.
  • Do not disturb other students.
  • Maintain silence when required.

📱 Digital Learning Rules

  • Use school website and mobile application responsibly.
  • Do not misuse digital facilities.
  • Protect login information.
  • Use online resources only for educational purposes.

🌱 Environment Rules

  • Keep school campus clean.
  • Save water and electricity.
  • Protect plants and trees.
  • Participate in environmental activities.

🤝 Behaviour Guidelines

  • Respect everyone equally.
  • Avoid bullying and harmful behaviour.
  • Maintain positive attitude.
  • Follow school instructions.

⭐ Our Goal

To create a safe, smart and positive learning environment where every student can grow with knowledge, discipline and confidence.
